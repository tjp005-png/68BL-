Base Business Classification Methodology

To identify the stable baseline volume of the facility, waste profiles are programmatically classified into one of the following categories based on shipment frequency:

Base Business (Multi-Year)

Profiles active in 3 or more calendar years (2023–2026). Represents the core, long-term baseline volume.

Base Business (Recurring)

Profiles active in 5 or more calendar months in at least 2 separate years. Represents high-frequency recurring business.

Base Business (New 2026)

Profiles showing newly sustained consistency in 2026, defined as active in 4 or more months in the current YTD classification window. These profiles are projected separately until they establish a longer history.

Non-Base (Campaign & Spot)

All other profiles. Typically short-term remediation projects, spot volumes, or ad-hoc shipments with low frequency.
